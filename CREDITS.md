# Sample credits

All bird and forest recordings come from Wikimedia Commons and are **public domain or CC0**,
so they can be shared freely. They were trimmed, high-pass filtered, faded and
peak-normalised for this project (`samples/`).

| file(s) | source | author | licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `blackbird/00–04` | [Turdus merula 2.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Turdus_merula_2.ogg) — Common Blackbird singing in a forest in Southern Finland | Oona Räisänen (Mysid) | Public domain |
| `blackbird/05` | [Blackbird slowed down.flac](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Blackbird_slowed_down.flac) — blackbird song slowed 10× | Drummyfish | CC0 |
| `nightingale/00–05` | [Nachtigall.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nachtigall.ogg) — singing Nightingale (*Luscinia megarhynchos*) | Membeth | CC0 |
| `rainforest/00` | [Walk in the rainforest.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Walk_in_the_rainforest.ogg) | ezwa | Public domain |
| `rainforest/01–02` | [Toucans singing in the Amazonian rainforest, Brazil](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:404114_felix-blume_toucans-singing-in-the-amazonian-rainforest-brazil.ogg) | Felix Blume | CC0 |

Drum breaks and hits are not stored here — Strudel loads them at play time from
[tidalcycles/Dirt-Samples](https://github.com/tidalcycles/Dirt-Samples).
Rhodes, strings and trumpet are General MIDI soundfonts built into Strudel; the sub bass is a sine wave.

The vocal in `samples/vocals/` is an original recording and is not covered by the licences above.
